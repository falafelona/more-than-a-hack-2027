| D14 | 2026-11-15 | **No `X` is written** anywhere (`x_policy: none` in `final.yaml`); every frame gets `H`, `A` or `D` | Ungraded frames are never scored, so `X` or a label there gives the same score. | D17 | 2026-11-11 | The dev set takes its truth from IDSSE's own `BallStatus` and `BallPossession`, and its ball is **cut to look like ours**: gaps with the gap-length bins measured on our match (ball EDA), fixed seed, placed in **two ways**: `random` (anywhere) and `stoppage` (starting as the ball goes dead). Every comparison runs under both. IDSSE lives in `IDSSE_DATA_DIR`, outside the repo, and humans run `scripts/devset_eval.py` (`make devset`) | The provider's flag gives per-frame H/A/D without matching events, and IDSSE's ball is nearly complete while ours is missing about 41%. Our real gaps are a mix of open-play losses and stoppages, so the two placements bracket them. The flag and the simulated gaps are only approximations, so the dev score compares versions and does not predict the real one | ✅ |
| D18 | 2026-11-15 | **Go / No-Go, as we applied it:** every change is scored against the current `final.yaml` in four dev-set runs (random and stoppage gaps × seeds 0 and 1). It goes into `final.yaml` only if it is better in all four runs, on at least 6 of 7 matches in each. A mean gain of at least +0.04 spellF1 per run passes outright. A smaller gain goes in only by an explicit team decision recorded in `RESULTS.md` and here (D22, D24, and D26 to D33 under D33). Each change lands as its own small PR with tests, an experiment config, the paired tables and a `RESULTS.md` row, approved by a reviewer. If nothing passes, `final.yaml` stays as it is | With 7 dev matches, an average gain can come from one lucky match; requiring the gain in every run, on almost every match, keeps only changes that hold up. Differences under 0.04 on one run are inside the noise, so they need a second look by people | ✅ |
| D19 | 2026-11-15 | **Still-ball clue** in the dead score: a seen ball standing still (under 0.5 m/s for 2 s) at a restart spot (near a touchline or goal line, the centre spot or the goal area) adds 0.6 to the chance the ball is dead | It caught set-up restarts the other clues missed (dev set +0.066 / +0.033 on top of #45, 7/7). On our match the ball mostly vanishes during stoppages, so it fires far less (about 930 frames); expect a smaller gain there | ✅ |
| D20 | 2026-11-15 | **Restart rule:** when a dead spell of at least 3 s ends, the team nearest the ball around that moment takes the restart; the other team pays an extra cost for 2 s | Every restart has a clear possessor (CHALLENGE.md). With the still-ball clue it gives +0.080 / +0.042 over #45 on both seeds, 7/7. Its threshold (0.5) is coupled to the still-ball weight (0.6): a weight below 0.5 would switch the rule off after those stoppages | ✅ |
| D21 | 2026-11-15 | **No hand-labelling** of any part of our match, for any purpose: training, tuning or validation | Forbidden by the competition rules. All labels used for choosing settings come with the IDSSE dataset; the public 5 minutes are only a pipeline check (D9) | ✅ |
| D22 | 2026-11-16 | **Switch placement:** each H / A switch is placed 75% of the way into its no-evidence stretch (`possession.switch_placement: 0.75`), not on its first frame | Where no team has evidence, H and A cost the same, so Viterbi leaves the switch at the earliest frame; in reality possession changes when the ball arrives.
