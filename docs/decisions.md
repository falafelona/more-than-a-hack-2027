| D13 | 2026-10-30 | The **H/A/D decoder is the core design**, and its main purpose is removing flicker. Dead ball is a score fed to it, not a hard override | spellF1 scores whole possessions; a false `D` splits one, so team and dead-ball decisions belong in one model | ✅ |
| D14 | 2026-11-15 | **No `X` is written** anywhere (`x_policy: none` in `final.yaml`); every frame gets `H`, `A` or `D` | Ungraded frames are never scored, so `X` or a label there gives the same score. On graded frames the scorer forward-fills `X` with our last label for spellF1, so `X` cannot gain, and it always counts as a miss for macroF1. Writing `X` on ungraded frames would only match the organisers' example file, for zero score change | ✅ |
| D15 | 2026-10-30 | All thresholds are **tuned on other matches**, starting with IDSSE | Hand labels and tuning on this match are banned, and organisers approved IDSSE (official issue #16) | ✅ |
| D16 | 2026-11-15 | Everyone keeps the match data on an **encrypted disk image, ejected while AI tools run** (`hdiutil detach /Volumes/mtah-data`) | Rules and ignore files only reduce risk; an unmounted disk cannot be read | ✅ |
| D17 | 2026-11-11 | The dev set takes its truth from IDSSE's own `BallStatus` and `BallPossession`, and its ball is **cut to look like ours**: gaps with the gap-length bins measured on our match (ball EDA), fixed seed, placed in **two ways**: `random` (anywhere) and `stoppage` (starting as the ball goes dead). Every comparison runs under both. IDSSE lives in `IDSSE_DATA_DIR`, outside the repo, and humans run `scripts/devset_eval.py` (`make devset`) | The provider's flag gives per-frame H/A/D without matching events, and IDSSE's ball is nearly complete while ours is missing about 41%. Our real gaps are a mix of open-play losses and stoppages, so the two placements bracket them. The flag and the simulated gaps are only approximations, so the dev score compares versions and does not predict the real one | ✅ |
| D18 | 2026-11-15 | **Go / No-Go:** a change goes into `final.yaml` only if it gains over the current `final.yaml` under both gap kinds (random, stoppage) and both seeds (0, 1), and is better on at least 6 of 7 dev matches in each of those four runs. It lands as its own small PR with tests, an experiment config, the four paired tables and a `RESULTS.md` row, approved by a reviewer. If nothing passes, `final.yaml` stays as it is | With 7 dev matches, an average gain can come from one lucky match; requiring the gain in every run, on almost every match, keeps only changes that hold up | ✅ |
| D19 | 2026-11-15 | **Still-ball clue** in the dead score: a seen ball standing still (under 0.5 m/s for 2 s) at a restart spot (near a touchline or goal line, the centre spot or the goal area) adds 0.6 to the chance the ball is dead | It caught set-up restarts the other clues missed (dev set +0.066 / +0.033 on top of #45, 7/7). On our match the ball mostly vanishes during stoppages, so it fires far less (about 930 frames); expect a smaller gain there | ✅ |
| D20 | 2026-11-15 | **Restart rule:** when a dead spell of at least 3 s ends, the team nearest the ball around that moment takes the restart; the other team pays an extra cost for 2 s | Every restart has a clear possessor (CHALLENGE.md). With the still-ball clue it gives +0.080 / +0.042 over #45 on both seeds, 7/7. Its threshold (0.5) is coupled to the still-ball weight (0.6): a weight below 0.5 would switch the rule off after those stoppages | ✅ |
| D21 | 2026-11-15 | **No hand-labelling** of any part of our match, for any purpose: training, tuning or validation | Forbidden by the competition rules. All labels used for choosing settings come with the IDSSE dataset; the public 5 minutes are only a pipeline check (D9) | ✅ |
